# Copyright and Content Policy

## Scope

The Watch Tracker Core repository contains franchise-independent software and documentation. Franchise-specific catalogs and editorial decisions belong in independently owned Canon Pack repositories.

## Contribution requirements

Contributors must submit only material they have the right to contribute under the repository license. Do not submit:

- posters, logos, stills, trailers, scripts, subtitles, or other protected media;
- copied synopses, reviews, biographies, or editorial explanations;
- scraped websites or database exports;
- material whose license or terms prohibit redistribution; or
- private, authenticated, or access-controlled data.

Short factual references required for interoperability may still involve trademark, contract, privacy, or jurisdiction-specific considerations. Record provenance and use the minimum material needed.

## Reports and removal

For a good-faith, non-legal concern, open a copyright-concern issue and identify the exact repository material and the rights involved. Do not publish personal information or confidential documents.

Formal notices concerning content hosted by GitHub may be submitted through [GitHub's DMCA process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

Maintainers may temporarily remove or disable disputed material while reviewing a credible report. Restoration requires a reasonable basis that the material may be distributed. Repeated submission of infringing material may result in loss of contribution privileges.
