# Watch Tracker Governance

## Purpose

Watch Tracker is intended to become a community-maintained project with a stable canonical repository, transparent decisions, and reviewable releases.

## Current model

During the foundation stage, `@bkswindell` is the repository owner and lead maintainer. The lead maintainer appoints additional maintainers, resolves decisions that do not reach consensus, and protects the canonical project identity and release channels.

This initial stewardship model is expected to evolve into a small maintainer council after multiple contributors have demonstrated sustained, constructive participation.

## Roles

### Contributors

Contributors propose issues, documentation, designs, code, tests, and reviews through GitHub. Contribution does not automatically grant merge, release, or administrative authority.

### Maintainers

Maintainers review changes, enforce project policies, manage issues, and protect scope and quality. Maintainers must disclose material conflicts of interest and must not approve their own substantive changes without another review when another maintainer is available.

### Lead maintainer

The lead maintainer manages repository access, appoints or removes maintainers, and makes final decisions when consensus is unavailable or an urgent security, legal, or operational response is required.

## Decision process

- Routine changes use pull-request review.
- Substantial interfaces, data contracts, governance changes, and scope changes require a public proposal or decision record.
- Maintainers seek rough consensus, not unanimity.
- Security and credible legal concerns may be handled privately and acted on immediately, with a public summary when appropriate.

## Canonical identity and forks

The Apache-2.0 license permits forks and commercial use. A fork must not imply that it is an official Watch Tracker release or use project marks in a way that suggests endorsement. The canonical repositories, official releases, domains, and project marks remain controlled by the project maintainers.

## Canon Pack independence

Each Canon Pack is independently owned and governed. Compatibility with Watch Tracker does not transfer ownership to the Core project or make Pack maintainers Core maintainers.

## Changing governance

Governance changes require a public pull request, an explanation of the impact, and approval by the lead maintainer while the foundation-stage model remains active.
