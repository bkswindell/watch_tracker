# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Watch Tracker community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and focus on the community's interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For project-specific, non-sensitive concerns, use the repository's conduct-concern issue form without publishing private information. Report sensitive project conduct privately to the [maintainer contact](mailto:brads@optbusinessservices.com). Platform abuse may also be reported through [GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Maintainers will review reports promptly and respect reporter privacy to the extent the available channel permits.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the project in public.
